## Overview

The $FAST staking system allows participants to stake tokens to any of the 21 active validators that secure the mev-commit preconfirmation protocol. Validators process preconfirmation commitments and are rewarded from protocol fees generated by FAST RPC and Fast Swaps activity.

Stakers delegate tokens to validators of their choice and receive a share of the validator's rewards, proportional to their stake and net of the validator's commission rate.

## How It Works

### Validators

Each validator in the $FAST staking set:

- Processes preconfirmation commitments on the mev-commit settlement chain
- Earns rewards from protocol fees (bid settlements, slashing proceeds)
- Sets a commission rate (typically 3–10%) deducted before distributing rewards to delegators
- Maintains uptime to remain in the active set

### Staking

To stake $FAST tokens:

1. **Connect your wallet** on [mev-commit.xyz/stake](https://mev-commit.xyz/stake)
2. **Choose a validator** based on staked amount, commission, APR, and uptime
3. **Enter the amount** of $FAST to delegate (minimum: 100 FAST)
4. **Confirm the transaction** to delegate your tokens

### Rewards

Staking rewards are derived from mev-commit protocol activity:

- **Source**: Preconfirmation bid fees, settlement processing, and slashing proceeds
- **Distribution**: Proportional to your share of the validator's total stake
- **Commission**: The validator's commission rate is applied before rewards reach delegators
- **Compounding**: Rewards can be re-staked to compound returns

### Unbonding

When you unstake (undelegate) your $FAST tokens:

- An **unbonding period of 7 days** begins
- During unbonding, tokens do not earn rewards
- After the unbonding period, tokens are returned to your wallet

## Validator Selection

When choosing a validator, consider:

| Factor | What to Look For |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Commission** | Lower commission means more rewards flow to you |
| **APR** | Higher APR indicates better reward efficiency |
| **Uptime** | 99.9%+ uptime indicates reliable infrastructure |
| **Stake concentration** | Distributing stake across validators improves decentralization |

## Security

The staking mechanism is secured by the mev-commit settlement chain smart contracts. Validators that fail to honor preconfirmation commitments are subject to slashing, which protects the integrity of the network and creates additional reward flow to honest participants.
