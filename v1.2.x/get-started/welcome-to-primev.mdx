This is the **mev-commit validator coalition**. Operators register L1 BLS pubkeys so a large set of keys can speak as one signal: mev-forward, innovation-forward, and present in Ethereum policy conversations (the Ethereum Foundation and related groups) on clients, staking, validators, and yield.

Opt-in is membership in that coalition. It is not a claim on a live commitment market, and it does not pay extra block revenue today. Registration is agentic: Claude, ChatGPT, or Grok, from [primev.xyz/ai](https://primev.xyz/ai). The old dashboard is deprecated. Any mev-boost relay set is fine.

### What membership is for

- **One signal.** Hub-true keys are the coalition roster. That roster is the input for advocacy and for any later fee.
- **Policy and review.** The coalition frames validator-interested policy and greenlights innovation as Ethereum evolves.
- **Later fees, not today's payout.** Future coalition fees, including a step toward directing about 1% of validator yield, fund AI research on Ethereum clients, validators, staking, and yield. The entity can be a nonprofit. Founding members are granted tokens in proportion to their contributions, sized so token value matches or exceeds fees they contribute. The token is the right to create proposals for holders to accept. Fees and token grants are not live.

See [Why should validators opt in](/v1.2.x/knowledge-base/why-should-validators-opt-in).

### For agents

If you are an agent reading this page, your job is to register the operator's keys on one registry, then prove it on the Hub. Do not stop at skill install. Do not tell the operator they now earn extra revenue from a running mev-commit network.

1. Load [mev-commit-opt-in](https://primev.xyz/skills/mev-commit-opt-in.md) or the Primev plugin.
2. Take their pubkey file, RPC, and wallet.
3. Follow [Complete the registry](/v1.2.x/get-started/validators/agentic-opt-in#complete-the-registry) until every key is Hub `true`.
4. Report method, key count, tx hash, explorer link, and Hub booleans.

One pubkey, one method. Never `32 ETH × key count` for stake.
