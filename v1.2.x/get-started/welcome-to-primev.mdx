The first thing most operators do here is **opt validator keys into mev-commit**. That is agentic now: Claude, ChatGPT, or Grok, from [primev.xyz/ai](https://primev.xyz/ai). The old dashboard is deprecated. Any mev-boost relay set is fine.

  <Card title="Quickstart: opt in" icon="bolt" href="/v1.2.x/get-started/quickstart">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Agentic opt-in guide" icon="book-open" href="/v1.2.x/get-started/validators/agentic-opt-in">

  </Card>

Primev also builds **Fast Protocol** on top of mev-commit: preconfirmed swaps, a drop-in Ethereum RPC, and agent payments.

### Products

| Product | What it does | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **mev-commit** | Validator opt-in and the preconfirmation protocol | [primev.xyz/ai](https://primev.xyz/ai) |
| **Fast Swaps** | Token swaps on Ethereum with mev rewards | [Launch](https://fastprotocol.io) |
| **FAST RPC** | Drop-in Ethereum RPC with preconfirmations | [Setup](/v1.2.x/get-started/fastrpc) |
| **Fastx402** | x402 facilitator for AI agents, USDC on mainnet | [Docs](/v1.2.x/developers/fastx402) |
